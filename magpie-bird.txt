Highly Intelligent: 
    Magpies are among the most intelligent animals in the world. They have demonstrated self-awareness in mirror tests, a trait shared by only a few species,
    including humans, dolphins, and elephants.

Tool Use: 
    Magpies have been observed using tools to obtain food, a behavior that highlights their problem-solving skills.

Mimicry: 
    Like parrots, magpies can mimic the sounds of other animals and even human speech. They use this ability to communicate and sometimes to confuse potential
    threats.

Strong Social Bonds: 
    Magpies are social birds that often live in family groups. They work together to raise young, protect their territory, and even engage incommunal activities
    like playing.

Symbolic in Various Cultures: 
    Magpies have various symbolic meanings in different cultures. In some Asian cultures, they are seen as harbingers of good fortune and happiness. In European
    folklore, they have a more mixed reputation, sometimes associated with superstition.

Collect Shiny Objects: 
    Magpies are often rumored to collect shiny objects like jewelry and coins. While this behavior has been somewhat exaggerated, they are curious and sometimes
    investigate and even take shiny items out of curiosity.

Complex Vocalizations: 
    Magpies have a wide range of vocalizations, including complex songs, calls, and even mimicry. They use these vocalizations for communication, attracting mates,
    and warding off intruders.

Long Lifespan: 
    In the wild, magpies can live up to 15 years, but in captivity, they have been known to live even longer, sometimes up to 20 years.

Memory Skills: 
    Magpies have excellent memories and can remember specific human faces. They are known to recognize and remember people who have either been kind or posed a threat
    to them in the past.

Symbolism in Literature: 
    Magpies often appear in literature and folklore. For instance, in the rhyme "One for Sorrow," the number of magpies seen is said to predict different kinds of
    fortunes.
